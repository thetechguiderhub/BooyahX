RULES

Please read all rules carefully before participating

General Rules

  • • All users must have a valid BooyahX account to participate in tournaments.
  • • Users must meet the minimum age requirement as per local laws.
  • • Each user may only have one account. Multiple accounts are not permitted.
  • • Users must provide accurate information during registration.
  • • BooyahX reserves the right to verify user identity at any time.

Tournament Rules

  • • Entry fees must be paid in full before the tournament starts.
  • • Players must join the match lobby at the specified start time.
  • • Late joins may result in disqualification at the organizer's discretion.
  • • All players must follow the specific tournament format and rules.
  • • Results are verified by the tournament organizer before publication.

CS Rules (4v4)

  • • Format: 4 vs 4 (Clash Squad)
  • • Each team must have exactly 4 players.
  • • Teams are responsible for their own coordination.
  • • Match rules follow standard competitive guidelines.
  • • Results are verified through match screenshots and lobby data.

BR Rules (48/49 Players)

  • • Format: 48/49 Player Lobby (Battle Royale)
  • • All players must join the lobby before the start time.
  • • Standard Battle Royale rules apply.
  • • Players must not leave the match once started.
  • • Results are verified through in-game placement data.

Fair Play

  • • No cheating or use of unauthorized third-party software.
  • • No exploiting bugs or glitches.
  • • No match manipulation or collusion.
  • • No harassment or abusive behavior towards other players.
  • • No hate speech, discriminatory language, or toxic behavior.
  • • Violations may result in permanent ban from the platform.

Anti-Cheating

BooyahX employs automated systems and manual review to detect suspicious activity. Any player found cheating will be immediately disqualified and may face permanent account suspension. Appeals can be submitted through the Support page.

Results

Results are verified and published after match completion. The verification process includes reviewing match data, screenshots, and lobby information. Results are typically published within 24 hours of match completion.

Cancellation

If a tournament is cancelled by the platform, all entry fees will be fully refunded to participants. Cancellations may occur due to insufficient players, technical issues, or other circumstances beyond our control.

Entry & Rewards

Entry fees are deducted from your coin balance upon joining a tournament. Rewards are distributed to eligible players after result verification. Reward amounts are clearly displayed before joining any tournament.

Withdrawal

Minimum withdrawal is 100 Coins. Withdrawal requests are reviewed and processed within 48-72 hours. Available withdrawal methods include Easypaisa and JazzCash. All withdrawals are subject to verification.

Frequently Asked Questions

How do I join a tournament? ▼
Sign in to your account, browse available tournaments, and click "Join Tournament" on any available event. Make sure you have sufficient BX Coins in your wallet.
How do I add BX Coins to my account? ▼
Go to your Wallet page, select a BX Coins package, choose your payment method (Easypaisa or JazzCash), and follow the payment instructions.
When will I receive my rewards? ▼
Rewards are distributed after tournament results are verified and published. This typically happens within 24 hours of match completion.
What is the minimum withdrawal amount? ▼
The minimum withdrawal amount is 100 BX Coins. Withdrawal requests are reviewed and processed within 48-72 hours.
